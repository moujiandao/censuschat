What blocks

Regression CI runs 6 stable scenarios twice. Every trial must pass. A fail, inconclusive result, or infrastructure error exits nonzero.

What reports

The 8 capability scenarios cover broader or less stable behavior. Behavioral fail and inconclusive outcomes remain visible without blocking, while infrastructure errors still fail the run.

Why inconclusive exists

It means the captured evidence cannot prove or disprove the claim. It remains non-passing and in the denominator, so uncertainty cannot inflate the score.

Grounding check boundary

The deterministic checker compares answer figures of at least four digits, excluding vintage years, only with captured query-row cells from the final turn. It does not infer arithmetic or lineage.

Artifacts stay separate

CI writes a GitHub artifact outside evals/results/. Plain make eval writes the timestamped and latest committed benchmark shown in the Evals tab.

No uncalibrated prose judge

Deterministic checks cover observable correctness. Clarity, caveat quality, and explanation quality remain human-reviewed until an LLM judge is calibrated against labeled examples.
